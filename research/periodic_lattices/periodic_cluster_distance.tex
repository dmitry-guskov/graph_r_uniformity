\noindent\textbf{Status.} This draft gives an analytic mixed-side classification, using the cited Hamming-graph code theorem for one base family. For dimension at least three, one side of length at least five suffices for full distance and singleton-only minima. All new finite checks are independent corroboration rather than proof dependencies. Publication novelty has not been established; the equal-side two-dimensional distance-five family is already in Ref.~\cite{kovalev2011}.

Let $G=\mathop{\square}_{i=1}^D C_{L_i}$, with vertex set $V=\prod_i\mathbb Z/L_i\mathbb Z$, and adjacency matrix $A$ over $\F$. The canonical graph-state generators are $K_v=X_v\prod_{u\sim v}Z_u$. For a nonempty generator subset $S$, the product $K_S=\prod_{v\in S}K_v$ has binary Pauli coordinates $(x,Ax)$, where $x=1_S$. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weight}
 W(S):=\wt(K_S)=|\supp(x)\cup\supp(Ax)|=|S\cup\Odd(S)|\ge |S|.
\end{equation}
Here $\Odd(S)$ contains vertices with an odd number of neighbors in $S$; vertices of $S$ remain in the support even when their $Z$ coordinates cancel.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
If $D\ge1$ and every $L_i\ge5$, then $\min_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq V}W(S)=2D+1$. The graph state is exactly $2D$-uniform. The bound applies to unequal side lengths.
\end{theorem}

\begin{lemma}[Two omitted consecutive slices]\label{lem:gaps}
Suppose that, in each coordinate, the projection of a nonempty $S$ omits two consecutive cycle positions. Then $W(S)\ge |S|+2D$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Relabel each coordinate so that positions $L_i-2,L_i-1$ are omitted. Thus $S$ lies in the integer box $\prod_i[0,L_i-3]$. For each $i$ choose $v_i^+,v_i^-\in S$ whose $i$th coordinates are respectively maximal and minimal, and set $q_i^+=v_i^++e_i$, $q_i^-=v_i^--e_i$, interpreted cyclically. Each $q_i^\pm$ is outside $S$ and has exactly one neighbor in $S$, namely $v_i^\pm$. Indeed, all neighbors obtained in another coordinate retain an $i$th coordinate outside the projection of $S$. The second neighbor in coordinate $i$ is beyond the corresponding extremum, or, on wrapping around the cycle, in one of the two omitted slices.

These $2D$ witnesses are distinct. The positive and negative witnesses in the same coordinate have different $i$th coordinates: if the extrema are $a\ge b$, their coincidence would require $a-b+2$ to be a multiple of $L_i$, whereas $2\le a-b+2\le L_i-1$. For $i\ne j$, witness $q_i^\pm$ has its $i$th coordinate outside the projection of $S$, while $q_j^\pm$ inherits that coordinate from a vertex of $S$. Therefore all witnesses lie in $\Odd(S)\setminus S$, proving the claim. This argument does not require $S$ to be connected.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Slice inequality]\label{lem:slices}
Write $G=H\square C_L$, and suppose every nonzero $y\in\F^{V(H)}$ satisfies $|\supp y\cup\supp(A_Hy)|\ge d_H$. For $x=1_S$, write $x_t$ for slice $t$, $r_t=|\supp x_t|$, $P=\{t:r_t>0\}$, and $k=|P|$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:slices}
 W(S)\ge k d_H-\sum_{u\in P}\deg_{C_L[P]}(u)r_u.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The $Z$ coordinate in slice $t$ is $A_Hx_t+x_{t-1}+x_{t+1}$. For any binary vectors $a,b,c$, perturbing $b$ to $b+c$ can remove at most $|\supp c|$ elements from $\supp a\cup\supp b$. For occupied $t$, therefore,
\[
 w_t:=|\supp x_t\cup\supp(A_Hx_t+x_{t-1}+x_{t+1})|
 \ge d_H-r_{t-1}-r_{t+1}.
\]
Sum over occupied $t$ and discard the nonnegative contributions of empty slices. Reindexing the subtracted terms gives~\eqref{eq:slices}. Cancellations may overlap or be counted more than once; this only weakens this lower bound and does not presume disjoint cancellation sites.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
A singleton $S$ has weight $2D+1$, so it suffices to rule out $W(S)\le2D$. Induct on $D$.

For $D=1$, a putative $W(S)\le2$ implies $1\le|S|\le2$. On $C_L$ with $L\ge5$ there are at least three empty positions. If no two empty positions were consecutive, the successor of every empty position would be occupied; the successor map is injective, so there would be at least as many occupied as empty positions. This contradicts $|S|\le2$. Lemma~\ref{lem:gaps} now gives $W(S)\ge|S|+2\ge3$.

For $D\ge2$, assume the theorem in dimension $D-1$ and let $r=|S|\le W(S)\le2D$. Fix any coordinate. Its slice graph $H$ satisfies $d_H=2D-1$ by induction. Let $P$ and $k$ be as in Lemma~\ref{lem:slices}.

If $k\ge5$, every induced degree is at most two, whence
\[
 W(S)\ge k(2D-1)-2r\ge5(2D-1)-4D=6D-5>2D.
\]
If $k=4$, then $P$ is a proper subset of a cycle of length at least five. Its induced graph is a nonempty disjoint union of paths, including possible isolated vertices. Hence $\sum_{u\in P}(2-\deg_{C_L[P]}u)\ge2$. Since each $r_u\ge1$, its weighted counterpart is also at least two. Thus
\[
 W(S)\ge4(2D-1)-(2r-2)\ge4D-2>2D.
\]
Therefore every coordinate projection has at most three occupied positions.

Suppose a coordinate projection has no two consecutive empty positions. Again the successor injection gives $L-k\le k$. Since $L\ge5$ and $k\le3$, this forces $k=3$ and $L\in\{5,6\}$. For $L=5$ the two empty positions are nonadjacent, and the occupied induced graph consists of one edge and one isolated vertex. For $L=6$, occupied and empty positions alternate, so every occupied induced degree is zero. In either case the induced maximum degree is at most one. The slice inequality yields
\[
 W(S)\ge3(2D-1)-r\ge4D-3>2D.
\]
All inequalities are strict also in the smallest induction dimension $D=2$: the three respective lower bounds are $7,6,5$, each exceeding $4$.

Consequently every coordinate has two consecutive empty slices, and Lemma~\ref{lem:gaps} gives $W(S)\ge r+2D\ge2D+1$, a contradiction. Finally, a graph-state reduced density matrix is maximally mixed on every set of at most $t$ qubits exactly when no nonidentity stabilizer has weight at most $t$, giving the uniformity statement.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Minimum-weight multiplicity]\label{cor:unique}
For $D\ge3$ and all $L_i\ge5$, the only stabilizer elements of weight $2D+1$ are the $N=\prod_i L_i$ individual canonical generators. In particular the minimum-weight stabilizer multiplicity is exactly $N$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Use the established theorem for the slice graphs and assume $W(S)\le2D+1$, so $r\le2D+1$. The preceding three projection bounds become respectively
\[
\begin{aligned}
 k\ge5 &:\quad W(S)\ge6D-7,\\
 k=4 &:\quad W(S)\ge4D-4,\\
 k\le3\text{ with no adjacent empties} &:\quad W(S)\ge4D-4.
\end{aligned}
\]
For $D\ge3$ all exceed $2D+1$ (at $D=3$ they are $11,8,8$, compared with $7$). Thus Lemma~\ref{lem:gaps} applies and $r+2D\le W(S)\le2D+1$, forcing $r=1$. Distinct generator subsets specify distinct stabilizer elements, so precisely $N$ elements attain this weight.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Why the dimension restriction matters.}
On $C_5\square C_5$, let $S=\{(t,t):t\in\mathbb Z/5\mathbb Z\}$. Every vertex has either zero or two neighbors in $S$, so $\Odd(S)=\varnothing$ and $W(S)=5$, although $|S|=5$. The two diagonal slopes and their five translates supply ten such stabilizers. The exhaustive calculation below confirms that these and the 25 individual generators are all the weight-five elements.

\begin{corollary}[Sharp universal common-side threshold]
The number five cannot be replaced by four in Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
On $C_4^{\square D}$ write $A=\sum_i(T_i+T_i^{-1})$ for the coordinate shifts over $\F$. These commute and $T_i^4=I$, so
\[
 A^2=\sum_i(T_i^2+T_i^{-2})=0.
\]
For $x=Ae_0$, the support consists of the $2D$ distinct neighbors of the origin, while $Ax=0$. Thus $W(\supp x)=2D$. This proves sharpness of a universal lower threshold for all side lengths; it does not classify tori with some side lengths four and others larger.
\end{proof}

\noindent\textbf{Reproducible finite checks.}
The accompanying C++ program computes $|x\lor Ax|$ by exact bit operations for every generator subset of each stated cardinality containing the origin. Translation transitivity implies that a minimum of any fixed cardinality occurs among these anchored subsets. Every subset of weight at most $b$ has cardinality at most $b$ by~\eqref{eq:weight}; there is no connectivity restriction. The number of enumerated anchored subsets must equal $\binom{N-1}{r-1}$. The raw log and structured JSON retain these counts, witnesses, minimizer counts, timings, compiler identity, and commands. For a vertex-transitive graph, an anchored minimizer count $a_r$ corresponds to $Na_r/r$ global minimizers, by counting incidences between minimizing subsets and their vertices.

For $C_5^3$ all $4{,}700{,}324{,}626$ anchored subsets of cardinalities one through seven were checked; the minimum weights by cardinality are $7,10,13,14,15,12,13$. This exhaustively verifies both distance seven and the 125-element minimum multiplicity in this instance. For $C_5^2$, cardinalities one through five have minima $5,6,7,8,5$, with one and two anchored minimizers in cardinalities one and five, respectively, giving 35 global weight-five elements. Checks for $C_5\square C_6$ and $C_5\square C_7$ exhaust cardinalities up to four. The $C_5^4$ check exhausts only cardinalities up to four and therefore is a partial stress test, not a finite certification of its distance nine.

\noindent\textbf{Literature position and unresolved priority.}
Sudevan et al.~\cite{sudevan2023}, Sec.~II.B and Appendix~C, prove the periodic cluster-state result for linear size at least eight and explicitly conjecture the threshold five from low-dimensional evidence. The October 2025 version of the subsequent code paper~\cite{sudevan2025}, Proposition~2 and Corollary~1, retains the side-length-at-least-eight hypothesis. The proof above, if independently accepted, resolves the cited arbitrary-dimensional threshold-five conjecture. The two-dimensional rectangular distance-five case and a diagonal minimum on the five-by-five torus already appear in Ref.~\cite{sudevan2022}. This is a mathematical comparison, not a confirmed novelty claim. In particular the 2019 abstract~\cite{lennen2019} explicitly mentions studying Cartesian products of diagonal distance without stating a usable theorem. The associated full result was not located in the bounded search; it remains a priority-check lead.
