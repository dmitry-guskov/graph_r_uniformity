# Focused priority challenge, 2026-09-06

Assessment: no inspected theorem directly implies the abstract long-factor stabilizer-distance lemma. This is a bounded negative search result, not evidence sufficient to claim priority. The equal-side two-dimensional graph-state distance result is explicitly known from Kovalev, Dumer and Pryadko (2011), as detailed below. Fish's 2017 journal paper still needs its actual full text inspected. Existing classical-code results already settle some all-short subfamilies and must be cited.

## Kovalev, Dumer and Pryadko: Design of additive quantum codes via the codeword-stabilized framework

Primary accepted manuscript: https://link.aps.org/accepted/10.1103/PhysRevA.84.062319 . Physical Review A 84, 062319, published 21 December 2011. Inspected the stabilizer-state distance definition (paper p. 3), Examples 5 and 6 (pp. 6-7), and their surrounding discussion.

Example 6 explicitly states graph-state distance five for C_L square C_L with L>=5. This is a direct prior result, not merely an analogous classical-code calculation.

Example 5's higher-dimensional sentence concerns CWS codes on finite open hypercubic fragments: code distance d=2D, with redundancy scaling with boundary area. The earlier paragraph excludes perimeter or corner vertices from the classical code. It does not assert graph-state distance d'=2D+1 on periodic D-tori, an unequal-side threshold, or minimum multiplicity. The distinction between the CWS-code distance d and the originating graph-state distance d' matters here.

Audit inference: the candidate contribution cannot be the equal-side D2 distance family. Its narrower possible scope is a proved unequal/mixed-side classification, the abstract long-cycle lifting lemma, the resulting arbitrary-dimensional single-long-side threshold, and minimum multiplicities. These possibilities still need a wider priority check.

## Fish, Key and Mwambene: Special LCD codes from products of graphs

Primary author manuscript: https://www.math.clemson.edu/~keyj/Key/FKM16JRev.pdf . Published in Applicable Algebra in Engineering, Communication and Computing 34 (2023), 553-579; DOI 10.1007/s00200-021-00517-4. Inspected the abstract, Theorem 1, and the complete Cartesian-product Section 3, manuscript pages 8-10.

Proposition 1 preserves the binary reflexive-LCD property under Cartesian products. For a simple graph this property is equivalent to A^2=A over F_2. Lemmas 4 and 5 construct Cartesian-product supports of codewords from component primal/dual codewords; these supply codeword existence and upper bounds. The remainder of Section 3 concerns ranks through eigenvalues. This section does not give our support-union lower bound for an h-regular cross-section times a long cycle.

Audit inference: C4 and cycles of length at least five fail A^2=A, so Proposition 1's RLCD-factor hypothesis does not cover our added cycle. The graph-state metric is the union support of a selection and its adjacency output; identifying it with primal/dual Hamming distances was valid for our all-triangle base specifically because that adjacency matrix is idempotent.

Inspected abstract and first-page preview only. The article studies binary adjacency row-span codes for powers of a common cycle, under Cartesian, categorical and lexicographic graph products. Publisher article/PDF retrieval failed; the indexed ResearchGate page exposes only a preview. No actual theorem from this journal paper is asserted to have been checked or excluded. The 2007 thesis below is a separately inspected antecedent, not a substitute claimed to be identical to the 2017 article.

## Fish: Codes from Uniform Subset Graphs and Cycle Products, PhD thesis, 2007

Primary institutional item: https://uwcscholar.uwc.ac.za/items/83398aba-1b3e-4f6c-a9fa-db63ce1e2673 . Full PDF: https://uwcscholar.uwc.ac.za/bitstreams/f6ba1ee1-d196-46aa-9c36-4bd0541bb325/download . Inspected Chapter 8, especially Lemma 8.2.3, Proposition 8.2.17 and Conjecture 8.2.18, printed pages 114-115 and 136-137 (PDF pages 125-126 and 147-148).

The text derives a self-dual binary adjacency code of distance 2D for C4^D. Proposition 8.2.17 gives adjacency-code parameters [8^D,6*8^(D-1),2D] for C8^D, containing its dual. Conjecture 8.2.18 proposes analogous adjacency-code parameters for power-of-two side lengths.

Audit inference: the C4 result already implies graph-state distance 2D, because Ax!=0 has weight at least 2D, while Ax=0 puts x in the same self-dual code; the neighborhood selection attains this bound. For C8, the stated distances and containment give graph-state distance at least 2D, not the desired 2D+1 directly. These equal-side results do not state the arbitrary-cross-section long-cycle lemma or classify unequal mixed-side tori.

## Remaining boundary

The complete 2017 article is the most concrete unresolved priority check. No matched existing lower-bound lemma was found by the targeted searches for local minimum degree, graph states and Cartesian products. The abstract lifting lemma should remain a candidate contribution until this gap and a wider graph-state/code literature check are resolved.

## Sudevan and Das, 2022: rectangular D2 antecedent

The primary arXiv abstract at https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.05622v2 explicitly includes periodic two-dimensional lattices with both dimensions at least five. The author-uploaded manuscript at https://www.researchgate.net/publication/357927139 gives the construction in its 4-entangled-states section and explicitly depicts a diagonal weight-five selection in Fig. 5(d). Thus neither unequal D2 sides at least five nor the existence of a diagonal minimum on C5 square C5 is a new claim. A complete minimum classification is a narrower question, and the short-side D2 strips and general-D single-long-factor theorem remain distinct candidate statements.
